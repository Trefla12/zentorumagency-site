How we worked this out
  • Everyone: hosting and domain at about $15 a month (typical range $5–$30).
  • DIY builder: a plan at about $25 a month (typical range $15–$40), included instead of hosting. About 20 hours to learn, write and build 1–5 pages, or 40 hours for a bigger site. About 1 hour a month to keep it up to date.
  • Freelancer: $1,500 for 1–5 pages or $3,500 for a bigger site, unless you enter your own quote. Real quotes vary a lot, from about $500 to $5,000 and up. About 8 or 12 hours of your time for briefs, writing and reviews. About 30 minutes a month to request changes.
  • Zentorum: our starting prices, $1,250 for 1–5 pages or $2,500 for 6–15 pages or a store. Your final quote comes after a call. About 3 or 5 hours of your time for one call and 2 review rounds. About 30 minutes a month to request changes.
  • These are estimates to help you think, not quotes. Your numbers may be different.

Straight answers

When each one is the right call

DIY builder

You have more time than budget

Just starting out, testing an idea or happy to learn? A builder is a fine first step. Keep it to a few pages, write clearly and use our free tools to check it.

Freelancer

You already know someone good

A freelancer you trust, with work you have seen, can be great value. Ask for a fixed quote, a timeline and who owns the files before you start.

Zentorum

You want it done right, and soon

A custom site, live in days, with a team you can call. You spend a few hours, not a few weekends. See a free mockup first.

Questions

Still deciding?

Can I start with a builder and switch later?

Yes. Many businesses do. Moving usually means rebuilding the design, but your words, photos and domain come with you. Keep your domain in your own name from day one.

Why is a freelancer not always cheaper?

The quote may be lower, but you often write the words, chase updates and handle hosting yourself. Add your hours and the gap can close.

Do you work with sites built on Wix or Squarespace?

Yes. We can review what you have with our free website teardown and tell you whether to keep it, fix it or rebuild.
